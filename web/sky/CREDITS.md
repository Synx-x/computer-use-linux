# Image credits

Every plate in this folder is a re-graded derivative of an openly licensed photograph. `tools/build_assets.py` rebuilds all three from the sources below.

## deep-field.webp

- Source: "The Milky Way panorama", ESO image eso0932a, 6000×3000 large JPEG.
- Page: https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/
- File: https://cdn.eso.org/images/large/eso0932a.jpg
- Credit: **ESO/S. Brunier**
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), per https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/
- Changes: stereographic reprojection around galactic lon 60°, lat −22° to 3300×3300 px, black point raised, graded toward mauve and blue, light unsharp mask, WebP q82.

## nebula-tarantula.webp

- Source: "Tarantula Nebula (NIRCam Image)", ESA/Webb image weic2212a, 4000×2313 publication JPEG.
- Page: https://esawebb.org/images/weic2212a/
- File: https://cdn.esawebb.org/archives/images/publicationjpg/weic2212a.jpg
- Credit: **NASA, ESA, CSA, and STScI**
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), per https://esawebb.org/copyright/
- Changes: cropped, resized to 2700×1735 px, luminance gradient-mapped to the near-black, indigo, mauve and lavender palette with part of the original colour kept. Soft elliptical alpha mask, WebP q80 with alpha.

## near-stars.webp

- Source: the same Tarantula frame at full resolution, 14557×8418 large JPEG.
- File: https://cdn.esawebb.org/archives/images/large/weic2212a.jpg
- Credit: **NASA, ESA, CSA, and STScI**
- Licence: CC BY 4.0, as above.
- Changes: 16 isolated stars cut at 1:1 with their diffraction spikes, background subtracted, colour mostly neutralised. Placed on a transparent 2800×1700 px sheet, WebP q88 with alpha.
